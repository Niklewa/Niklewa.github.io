Cognitive biases influence about 75% of strategic decisions, with overconfidence being a major cause of costly errors like failed mergers, missed opportunities, and sunk projects.

Business Giants Did It — So Can You

Blockbuster & Netflix
Overconfidence & Confirmation Bias
Blockbuster thought they were too strong to worry about Netflix and ignored new ideas. Netflix grew fast; Blockbuster went out of business.
Kodak & Digital Photography
Status Quo Bias & Loss Aversion
Kodak stuck with film cameras and didn’t want to change. They ignored digital cameras and lost their business.
BlackBerry & Touchscreens
Overconfidence & Confirmation Bias
BlackBerry believed people would always want keyboards and ignored touchscreens. They quickly lost their lead.
Yahoo & Google
Anchoring Bias & Underestimation
Yahoo focused on their old way of doing things and didn’t see how good Google was. They missed a big chance to buy Google.
